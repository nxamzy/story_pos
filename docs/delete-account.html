Ocam POS — hisobni o'chirish

Ilova: Ocam POS  ·  Paket nomi: uz.ocam.pos

Ocam POS hisobingizni va u bilan bog'liq barcha ma'lumotni o'chirishning ikki yo'li bor.

1-usul — ilova ichidan (tavsiya etiladi)

  1. Ocam POS ilovasini oching va hisobingizga kiring
  2. Profil → Sozlamalar bo'limiga o'ting
  3. Pastdagi "Xavfli hudud" bo'limini toping
  4. "Hisobni butunlay o'chirish" tugmasini bosing
  5. Ogohlantirishni o'qib, tasdiqlang
  6. Parolingizni kiriting

Hisob va ma'lumot darhol o'chiriladi. Tasdiqlash xatining kutilishi shart emas.

2-usul — email orqali so'rov

Ilovaga kira olmasangiz (masalan telefonni yo'qotgan bo'lsangiz), quyidagi manzilga yozing:

Kimga: jamshidbekormonjonov@gmail.com

Mavzu: Hisobni o'chirish so'rovi

Xat matnida: hisobingiz ro'yxatdan o'tgan email manzilni yozing. Xatni o'sha manzilning o'zidan yuboring — shu orqali hisob egasi ekaningiz tasdiqlanadi.

So'rov 30 kun ichida bajariladi va bajarilgani haqida javob xati yuboriladi.

Hech qanday ma'lumot zaxira nusxa sifatida saqlab qolinmaydi. Serverdagi avtomatik texnik nusxalar (Google Firebase infratuzilmasi) eng ko'pi bilan 30 kun ichida o'zi yo'qoladi.

Diqqat: bu amalni ortga qaytarib bo'lmaydi. Savdo tarixingiz yoki ombor ma'lumotingiz keyinchalik kerak bo'lishi mumkin deb hisoblasangiz, o'chirishdan oldin kerakli cheklarni chop etib yoki PDF qilib saqlab oling.

Ocam POS — delete your account

App: Ocam POS  ·  Package name: uz.ocam.pos

There are two ways to delete your Ocam POS account and all data associated with it.

Option 1 — inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Ocam POS and sign in
  2. Go to Profil → Sozlamalar (Settings)
  3. Scroll to "Xavfli hudud" (Danger zone)
  4. Tap "Hisobni butunlay o'chirish" (Delete account permanently)
  5. Read the warning and confirm
  6. Enter your password

The account and data are deleted immediately. No confirmation email is required.

Option 2 — request by email

If you cannot access the app (for example, you lost your phone), write to:

To: jamshidbekormonjonov@gmail.com

Subject: Account deletion request

In the message: state the email address your account is registered with. Send the message from that same address so we can verify ownership.

Requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed by reply.
