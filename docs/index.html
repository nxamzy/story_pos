Ocam POS — Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 4-sentabr

Ocam POS — do'konlar uchun kassa, ombor va hisobot dasturi. Bu hujjat ilova qanday ma'lumot yig'ishini, uni nima uchun ishlatishini, qayerda saqlashini va uni qanday o'chirishingiz mumkinligini tushuntiradi.

Qisqacha: ilova reklama ko'rsatmaydi, kuzatuv (analytics) vositalaridan foydalanmaydi va ma'lumotingizni hech kimga sotmaydi hamda uchinchi shaxsga bermaydi. Kiritgan hamma narsangiz faqat sizning hisobingizga bog'langan va faqat siz ko'ra olasiz. Hisobni istagan vaqtda ilova ichidan butunlay o'chirishingiz mumkin.

1. Qanday ma'lumot yig'iladi

1.1. Hisob ma'lumoti

Ma'lumotNima uchun
Email manzilHisobga kirish va parolni tiklash
ParolGoogle Firebase Authentication tomonidan shifrlangan holda saqlanadi. Biz parolingizni ko'ra olmaymiz.
Ism va familiyaIlovada va chekda ko'rsatish

1.2. Do'kon ma'lumoti

Sozlamalarda kiritiladi va chekda chop etiladi: do'kon nomi, aloqa raqami, manzil, STIR, valyuta hamda qurilma sozlamalari (chek qog'ozi o'lchami, skaner tebranishi, ogohlantirish chegarasi, vaqt formati).

1.3. Siz kiritadigan ish ma'lumoti

Ilovaning asosiy vazifasi shu ma'lumotni saqlash va hisoblashdir:

  • Mahsulotlar — nomi, shtrix-kodi, sotib olish va sotish narxi, qoldiq, kategoriya, tavsif
  • Mijozlar — ismi, telefon raqamlari, email, manzil, eslatma, jami xarid summasi
  • Ta'minotchilar — nomi, telefon, email, manzil, eslatma
  • Xodimlar — ismi, lavozimi, telefon raqamlari, oylik, balans, davomat, eslatma va kassir PIN kodining sha256 xeshi (PIN ochiq matnda saqlanmaydi)
  • Savdolar va qaytarishlar — mahsulotlar, summalar, to'lov turi, mijoz va kassir nomi, sana
  • Xaridlar, xarajatlar, kassa balansi va o'tkazmalar tarixi
Muhim: mijoz, ta'minotchi va xodim ma'lumotini ilovaga siz kiritasiz. Boshqa odamlarning shaxsiy ma'lumotini kiritish va saqlash uchun qonuniy asosga ega bo'lish sizning javobgarligingizda.

1.4. Kamera

Ilova shtrix-kodni o'qish uchun kameradan foydalanadi. Kamera tasviri saqlanmaydi va hech qayerga yuborilmaydi — undan faqat kodning raqami ajratib olinadi.

1.5. Yig'ilmaydigan ma'lumot

  • Joylashuv (GPS)
  • Telefon kontaktlari
  • Galereya, rasm va videolar
  • SMS va qo'ng'iroqlar tarixi
  • Reklama identifikatori, kuzatuv yoki foydalanish statistikasi

2. Ma'lumot qayerda saqlanadi

Barcha ma'lumot Google'ning Firebase xizmatida (Cloud Firestore va Firebase Authentication) saqlanadi. Serverlar Amerika Qo'shma Shtatlarida joylashgan (nam5 mintaqasi). Ilovadan serverga uzatiladigan hamma narsa HTTPS orqali shifrlangan holda yuboriladi.

Google bu yerda bizning nomimizdan ma'lumotni qayta ishlovchi sifatida ishlaydi. Google Firebase maxfiylik shartlari: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Ma'lumotga kim kira oladi

Har bir do'konning ma'lumoti server qoidalari bilan himoyalangan: hujjatlarga faqat o'sha hisobning egasi kira oladi. Boshqa foydalanuvchi sizning mahsulotingizni, savdongizni, mijozingizni yoki kassa balansingizni ko'ra olmaydi.

Ma'lumotingiz reklama maqsadida ishlatilmaydi, sotilmaydi va uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi.

4. Kassir PIN kodi haqida

Xodimga o'rnatiladigan PIN kod ochiq matnda saqlanmaydi — faqat uning sha256 xeshi saqlanadi va har bir xodim uchun alohida "tuz" (xodim identifikatori) qo'shiladi. Bu PIN himoya emas, imzo: u savdo kim tomonidan qilinganini chekka va hisobotga yozish uchun ishlatiladi. Qurilmadagi barcha ma'lumot baribir do'kon egasining hisobiga tegishli.

5. Ma'lumot qancha saqlanadi

Siz hisobingizni o'chirmaguningizcha. Hisob o'chirilgan zahoti do'koningizning barcha hujjatlari (mahsulot, savdo, mijoz, ta'minotchi, xodim, xarajat, xarid, kassa) serverdan butunlay o'chiriladi.

6. Hisobni va ma'lumotni o'chirish

Ilova ichidan: Sozlamalar → "Xavfli hudud" → "Hisobni butunlay o'chirish". Parolingiz qayta so'raladi. Tasdiqlagach ma'lumot va hisob darhol o'chiriladi.

Ilovasiz: hisobni o'chirish sahifasidagi ko'rsatma bo'yicha so'rov yuboring.

Bu amalni ortga qaytarib bo'lmaydi.

7. Bolalar

Ocam POS — tadbirkorlar uchun mo'ljallangan ish dasturi. U 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalar uchun mo'ljallanmagan va ulardan ataylab ma'lumot yig'maydi.

8. Sizning huquqlaringiz

  • Ma'lumotingizni ko'rish va tahrirlash — ilovaning o'zida
  • Ma'lumotingizni o'chirish — yuqoridagi 6-bo'lim
  • Savol berish yoki shikoyat qilish — quyidagi manzil orqali

9. Siyosatdagi o'zgarishlar

Ushbu hujjat o'zgarsa, yuqoridagi "Oxirgi yangilanish" sanasi yangilanadi. Muhim o'zgarishlar haqida ilova orqali xabar beriladi.

Ocam POS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Ocam POS is a point-of-sale, inventory and reporting app for shops. This document explains what data the app collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

In short: the app shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking SDKs, and never sells or shares your data with third parties. Everything you enter is tied to your own account and visible only to you. You can permanently delete your account from inside the app at any time.

1. Data we collect

1.1. Account data

DataPurpose
Email addressSign-in and password recovery
PasswordStored encrypted by Google Firebase Authentication. We cannot read it.
First and last nameShown in the app and on receipts
Phone number (optional)Profile information

1.2. Shop details

Entered in Settings and printed on receipts: shop name, contact phone, address, tax ID (STIR), currency, and device preferences (receipt paper size, scanner haptics, low-stock threshold, time format).

1.3. Business data you enter

Storing and calculating this data is the core purpose of the app:

  • Products — name, barcode, buy and sell price, stock, category, description
  • Customers — name, phone numbers, email, address, notes, total spent
  • Suppliers — name, phone, email, address, notes
  • Employees — name, role, phone numbers, salary, balance, attendance, notes, and a sha256 hash of the cashier PIN (the PIN itself is never stored)
  • Sales and refunds — items, totals, payment method, customer and cashier name, date
  • Purchases, expenses, cash drawer balance and transfer history
Important: customer, supplier and employee details are entered by you. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to store other people's personal data in the app.

1.4. Camera

The app uses the camera to scan barcodes. Camera images are never stored or transmitted — only the barcode number is extracted.

1.5. Data we do not collect

  • Location (GPS)
  • Phone contacts
  • Photos, media or files
  • SMS or call logs
  • Advertising ID, tracking or usage analytics

2. Where data is stored

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Servers are located in the United States (nam5 multi-region). All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.

Google acts as a data processor on our behalf. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. Who can access your data

Server-side security rules restrict every document to the account that owns it. No other user can see your products, sales, customers or cash balance. Your data is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties.

4. About the cashier PIN

Employee PINs are never stored in plain text — only a sha256 hash, salted per employee. The PIN is attribution, not authorisation: it records who made a sale on the receipt and in reports. All data on the device still belongs to the shop owner's account.

5. Retention

Until you delete your account. When you do, every document belonging to your shop (products, sales, customers, suppliers, employees, expenses, purchases, cash drawer) is permanently removed from the server.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → "Xavfli hudud" (Danger zone) → "Hisobni butunlay o'chirish" (Delete account permanently). Your password is requested again; after confirmation the data and account are deleted immediately.

Without the app: follow the instructions on the account deletion page.

This action cannot be undone.

7. Children

Ocam POS is a business tool for shop owners. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Your rights

  • Access and edit your data — directly in the app
  • Delete your data — see section 6
  • Ask a question or raise a complaint — at the address below

If this document changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
