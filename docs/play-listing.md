### O'zbekcha

```
Ocam POS — kichik va o'rta do'konlar uchun to'liq savdo nuqtasi dasturi.
Kassa, ombor, mijozlar, ta'minotchilar, xarajatlar va hisobot bitta
ilovada, o'zbek tilida.

SAVDO VA CHEK
• Mahsulotni ro'yxatdan tanlang yoki shtrix-kodini kamerada skanerlang
• Savatda miqdorni o'zgartiring — ombordagi qoldiqdan ko'p qo'shilmaydi
• Naqd yoki karta to'lovi, olingan summadan qaytim avtomatik hisoblanadi
• Chekni 58 mm, 80 mm lentali printerda yoki A4 varaqda chop eting

OMBOR

MIJOZLAR

TA'MINOTCHILAR VA XARIDLAR

KASSA
```

### English

```
Ocam POS is a complete point-of-sale app for small and medium shops.
Checkout, inventory, customers, suppliers, expenses and reports in one
app.

SALES AND RECEIPTS
• Pick a product from the list or scan its barcode with the camera
• Adjust quantities in the cart — you cannot oversell your stock
• Cash or card payment, with change calculated automatically
• Print receipts on 58 mm or 80 mm thermal paper, or A4
• Receipts show shop name, address, tax ID, cashier and customer
• Sales can be backdated

INVENTORY
• Name, barcode, buy price, sell price and stock for every product
• Filter by category, search by name or barcode
• Low-stock warnings at a threshold you choose

CUSTOMERS
• Customer card with phone, email, address and notes
• Purchase history and lifetime spend per customer
• One-tap call, SMS or share
• Ranking of your highest-spending customers

SUPPLIERS AND PURCHASES
• Supplier list with contact details
• Stock-in raises inventory and updates the cost price
• Purchases can be paid from the cash drawer

CASH DRAWER
• Cash sales increase the drawer balance
• Transfer money between the drawer and employees, with full history
• Record expenses paid from the drawer

EMPLOYEES
• Employee card with role, salary, balance and attendance
• Cashier PIN records who made each sale on the receipt
• PINs are never stored in plain text

REFUNDS AND HISTORY
• Refund a sale: stock is restored and cash returns to the drawer
• Sales history searchable by date range, customer, cashier or
  receipt number — find and reprint any old receipt

REPORTS
• Daily, weekly and monthly sales, profit and profit after expenses
• Best-selling products
• Refunded sales are excluded from every total

DATA SAFETY
• Data is stored in Google Firebase over an encrypted connection
• Each shop's data is visible only to its own account
• No ads, no tracking, no sharing with third parties
• You can permanently delete your account from inside the app

An internet connection is required.
```

### Skrinshot rejasi (shu tartibda)

1. **Bosh sahifa** — kunlik savdo va menyu kartochkalari. Birinchi
   skrinshot eng muhimi: odam shuni ko'rib qaror qiladi.
2. **Savdo ekrani** — mahsulotlar ro'yxati va savat tugmasi
3. **Shtrix-kod skaneri** — kamera ochiq holda (eng "sotiladigan" ekran)
4. **Savat / To'lov** — summa, qaytim, to'lov turi
5. **Chek** — chop etishga tayyor chek ko'rinishi
6. **Ombor** — mahsulotlar ro'yxati, qoldiq bilan
7. **Hisobot** — foyda va eng ko'p sotilgan mahsulotlar
8. **Kassa** — balans va o'tkazmalar

**Muhim:** skrinshot olishdan oldin ilovaga **haqiqiyga o'xshash
ma'lumot** kiriting — 8-10 ta mahsulot, bir nechta mijoz va savdo. Bo'sh
ekranli skrinshot ilovani tugallanmagandek ko'rsatadi.

Emulyatorda olish:
```bash
flutter run --release
# keyin emulyatorda: Cmd+S (Android Studio emulyatori surat saqlaydi)
```
